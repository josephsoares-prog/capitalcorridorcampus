Parking in downtown Gatineau — the complete guide

Meters, City monthly permits, accessible parking and the bus: the options, checked against the official sources.

Meters, City monthly permits, accessible parking and the bus: the options, checked against the official sources.

The short answer: downtown Gatineau gives you three options. City meters suit short visits. A City monthly permit reserves an on-street space in the Hull sector for $146 or $168 a month. An STO bus pass costs $116.50 a month, and it is also valid on OC Transpo in Ottawa.

Paying at the meter

The Ville de Gatineau manages on-street parking downtown. Paying at a meter takes three steps: note your licence plate, enter the plate and the time you want, then pay. Source: Ville de Gatineau, Municipal parking.

Rates, time limits and paid hours vary by street. Check the signs on site before you walk away.

The reserved on-street monthly permit

If you come in every day, a monthly permit costs less than the meter. The City sells permits for reserved spaces on ten streets in the Hull sector. They are valid from March 1, 2026 to February 28, 2027.

  • $146 a month: rue Crémazie, Ducharme, Hanson, Joffre, Lois and Mutchmore, and two zones of rue Notre-Dame-de-l'Île/Champlain.
  • $168 a month: rue Marston and rue Saint-Rédempteur.

In these zones, parking is free after 5 p.m. on weekdays, on weekends and on holidays. To get a permit, fill in the City's "Demande de permis mensuel" form. Source: Ville de Gatineau.

Accessible parking

In Quebec, the disabled parking permit is issued by the SAAQ. Any person with a disability can get one, whether or not they live in Quebec, and whether they drive or ride as a passenger. You apply by mail. It takes four to six weeks and costs $20.10. Source: SAAQ.

Coming without a car: the STO

For a daily commute, the bus often costs less than a parking space. In 2026, an adult STO fare costs $4.75 in cash or $4.00 with a Multi card. The monthly pass costs $116.50. Source: STO, regular fares.

The regular STO monthly pass is also valid on OC Transpo. You can cross into Ottawa without buying a second fare. Source: STO.

What about the tramway?

The Gatineau-Ottawa tramway will not change anything soon. On the project's official timeline, the construction contract is to be awarded in 2030 and public opening is planned for 2035. Source: Gatineau-Ottawa Tramway, key milestones.

Winter

In winter, overnight on-street parking is banned, except for holders of a general winter overnight parking permit. When the City issues a winter parking ban notice, it is banned for everyone. Follow the temporary signs. Source: Ville de Gatineau, Snow clearing.
