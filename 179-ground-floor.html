This exceptional street-level commercial space represents a unique opportunity in Gatineau's most vibrant business corridor. With the federal government workforce returning to the office and foot traffic recovering on Promenade du Portage, this is the strategic moment for service-oriented businesses to establish their presence. Located steps away from Place du Portage—home to 10,000+ federal employees—this property captures premium walk-in traffic and clientele density unmatched elsewhere in the region.
