What Is a Virtual Office?

A virtual office gives you a professional business address and the services that come with it — mail handling, a phone line, and meeting room access — without the fixed costs of a traditional commercial lease. You get the credibility of an established address and a professional point of contact while working from wherever you choose.

In practical terms, a virtual office at 179 Promenade du Portage lets you use a prestigious address for business registration, correspondence, business cards, and official documentation. Your mail is received and managed on site, your calls are forwarded, and you book a private office or meeting room only when you need one.

For a professional in the Ottawa-Gatineau corridor — a consultant, federal contractor, legal practice, or policy institute — it is the most economical way to establish a serious presence next to government without tying up capital in office space.
